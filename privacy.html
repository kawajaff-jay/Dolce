Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Dolce+ app and website collect, why, and what choices you have. Dolce+ is operated by Dolce Clinic in Erbil, Kurdistan Region of Iraq, for its three businesses: Dolce Aesthetic Clinic, Polished by Dolce (hair & nail salon) and CORE Yoga & Pilates Studio. In this policy, "we" and "us" means Dolce Clinic.

1. What we collect

You can browse Dolce+ as a guest without giving us any personal information. We only collect information when you create an account, request a booking or place an order.

Because Dolce is an aesthetic clinic, the treatments you book may reveal information about your health or appearance. We treat booking information as sensitive and use it only to provide your appointment.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, browsing history or advertising identifier, and the app does not contain advertising or tracking tools. The in-app assistant works on your phone and does not send what you type to us.

2. How we use it

  • To log you in securely with a one-time code.
  • To receive, confirm, change or cancel your bookings and orders.
  • To contact you about your appointments, for example confirmations and reminders by WhatsApp, and aftercare advice if you agreed to it.
  • To run the D+ loyalty programme: keeping your points balance and level across all three brands.
  • To keep business records, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your information, use it for targeted advertising, or share it with data brokers.

3. Who can see it

  • Our staff, only as needed for their work. Staff accounts are limited to the brands and sections they work in.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us: our cloud database and login provider (Supabase), which stores the information on secure servers that may be located outside Iraq; Meta (WhatsApp), which delivers login codes and appointment messages; and the email service that delivers email login codes.
  • CORE's class schedule is read from CORE's Mindbody account. Dolce+ does not send your personal information to Mindbody. If you open CORE's Mindbody booking page, Mindbody's own privacy policy applies there.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is open. Booking and visit records are kept as business records for as long as we reasonably need them for our records and legal obligations.

5. Your choices

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete my account. This removes your login, name, phone number and points. Past appointments stay in our diary as business records.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us below.
  • Tell us at any time if you no longer want aftercare or other messages.

6. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, access is limited by login and permissions, and staff can only see what their role needs. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

7. Children

Dolce+ is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
