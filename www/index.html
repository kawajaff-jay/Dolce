Full data export

Exports every booking, product, service, offer and (if you manage Employees) employee account this login can see — respecting department access the same way the tabs above do. Useful for backups, or handing everything to an accountant or a new system.

"Export PDF" opens your browser's print dialog with a clean, print-ready version of the report — choose "Save as PDF" as the destination. This works fully offline and needs no extra software.
Backup & restore

Your database provider keeps no backups on the free plan — if records are deleted by mistake there is nothing on their side to recover. This saves a complete copy of everything to a file you keep yourself, and can put it all back later. Take one weekly, and keep it somewhere that isn't this computer.

Unlike the export above, a backup ignores department limits and includes everything, so only an owner can take or restore one. Staff logins live with the login provider and are not included — after a restore your team may need new PINs.
